[[Epistemic democracy]]
\[\[]]

> [!NOTE]
> A stagnant philosophy (one you haven't revised in three years, one that feels settled and complete ) isn't a sign of intellectual maturity.
> It's a sign that at some point you stopped exploring and started defending.
> The pride in philosophy is _not_ that you never changed your mind.
> If anything, its the opposite:
> The pride is in being willing to be wrong,
> to have the floor fall out,
> to sit with the discomfort of not knowing and keep digging anyway.
